Team headshots go in this folder.

1. Drop a file here, e.g.  herman.jpg
2. Open  lib/content.js  and find the member in the TEAM array
3. Change   photo: null   to   photo: '/team/herman.jpg'

Until you do that, the card shows an initials placeholder instead.

Best results with a 4:5 portrait crop (e.g. 800 x 1000 px). The card crops
to fill, so anything roughly portrait will work.
